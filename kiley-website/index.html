Profile

Design leadership for products people run their businesses on.

Kiley Daniel Meehan is a strategic product and design leader. At FreshBooks, he led experience strategy across accounting, payments, platform, and operations — one connected product, not four disconnected ones.

He owned the company’s Experience Vision and Principles, built the “Pathways” framework for aligning product teams around a single customer journey, and championed structured measurement — the HEART framework — over guesswork.

His mission, stated plainly on every strategy deck he shipped: Making the Complex Simple.
